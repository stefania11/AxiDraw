A4 paper & home position

  1. Release XY motors. When idle, use Raise pen & release XY in Calibration. Never force a locked carriage.
  2. Home the head. Gently move the solid carriage block fully left and back, near the Home label and USB port. Do not push the pen slide.
  3. Position the paper. Place its upper-left corner directly under the raised tip: 297 mm right, 210 mm toward you. Keep the rear rods and cables clear.
  4. Set the pen. Leave 3-6 mm clearance when raised. Lowered, the tip rests on the paper by its own weight.

Every plot assumes this corner is (0,0). The machine does not sense Home. Do not start from the center of the paper or from the first stroke.
